Roadmap to Magic · 30 books, their magic, and the machines that caught up

Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic.

Arthur C. Clarke, third law, Profiles of the Future (1973 edition)

Writers have spent two centuries imagining what wands, stones and starships could do. Engineers have quietly been checking items off the list. A self-writing quill is a dictation app. Tom Riddle's diary is a chatbot. Teleportation is still a spell.

Name a fantasy or science-fiction book that's missing. Anton, Andor's AI agent, reads up on it, writes down its magic and how close today's technology gets, and adds it to the roadmap. It takes about a minute.

Verdicts reflect public technology as of late 2026 and are judgment calls, not lab results. "Almost" means it exists in a narrower, clumsier or prototype form. "Not yet" means no known physics forbids it but nobody has built it. "Still magic" means it breaks physics as we understand it.
