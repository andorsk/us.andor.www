Night Market

The rule book

2–6 players (best with 3–5)About 20 minutesBluffing · deduction

IThe idea

You run a stall in a lantern-lit night market. Behind your counter are two hidden character cards, the people who work for you. Each character has a power, and on your turn you may claim any character's power, whether or not you actually hold that card.

Anyone may call your claim. Get caught bluffing and you pay a fine. Call an honest claim and you pay.

The market is open for 6 hours (6 rounds). When the last lantern burns out, the stallholder with the most coins wins. Nobody is ever knocked out; every coin counts until the end.

IIThe cast

The deck has 18 cards: three of each of these six characters.

IIISetting up

  1. One player opens a table and shares the invite link. Friends join with just a name.
  2. The host opens the market. Seating is shuffled.
  3. Everyone gets 2 hidden cards and 3 coins. The lantern pot starts empty.

IVYour turn

  1. Pay the stall fee. 1 coin goes from you into the lantern pot (if you have any coins). This happens automatically.
  2. Choose one move:
    • Haggle: take 2 coins from the bank. This is not a claim, so nobody can call it.
    • Claim a character: announce one of the six characters and use its power. You don't have to hold it.
  3. If you claimed a character, everyone else gets 12 seconds to call it or let it pass. If everyone lets it pass, or time runs out, your power happens.

You have 90 seconds for a turn. If you doze off, you automatically Haggle.

VCalling a bluff

The first player to press Call it! challenges the claim. The market checks the claimer's hidden cards:

What happens
The claimer was bluffingThe claim fails, and the claimer pays the caller 2 coins (or everything they have).
The claimer was honestThe caller pays the claimer 2 coins. The claimer's proven card is shuffled into the deck and they draw a fresh one, so everyone knows what they had, not what they have. Then the power happens as normal.
Haggle can never be called. Only character claims can.

VIThe powers in detail

  • Peddler: take 3 coins from the bank.
  • Pickpocket: choose a stallholder and take up to 2 of their coins.
  • Lamplighter: take every coin in the lantern pot. The pot grows by 1 every turn, so the longer nobody claims the Lamplighter, the bigger the prize.
  • Fortune-teller: choose a stallholder and name a character. If they hold at least one card of it, they pay you 3. If not, you pay them 1. The answer is announced to the table.
  • Fence: choose a stallholder and one of your cards. You give them that card and take one of theirs at random. Only the two of you learn which cards moved.
  • Ferryman: draw 2 cards from the deck, look at all 4, keep any 2 and return the other 2. Nobody else sees what you kept.

VIIEnd of the night

Each time play returns to the first seat, an hour passes and a lantern goes dark. After the 6th hour the market closes. Most coins wins; ties share the win. Coins left in the lantern pot belong to nobody.

The host can call a rematch with the same table, and new friends can join between games.

VIIITips from the old stallholders

  • Claims are cheap, calls are risky. A caller who's wrong pays the claimer and still has to watch the power land.
  • After an honest claim is called, the claimer has drawn a new card. Use that.
  • The Fortune-teller rewards memory: who has claimed what, who was called, and what moved under the table.
  • Watch the lantern pot. When it gets fat, everyone suddenly "has" a Lamplighter.
  • Haggling is safe but slow. You won't win the night on 2 coins a turn.
