1953 · A geochemistry lab in Pasadena

Take the Lead Out

Lead in petrol is poisoning everyone, especially children. Ban it now, not in 2021.

The young geochemist was trying to measure the age of the Earth, and his numbers would not behave. Every sample he took was contaminated with lead: from the air, the dust, his glassware, his own hands. It took him years to build a lab clean enough to find the truth, and in doing it, he found a second truth he never stopped talking about.

There was lead everywhere because people were putting it there. Since the 1920s, a lead compound had been added to petrol to stop engines knocking. Every car exhaust breathed it into the air, and it settled onto streets, playgrounds, and into children.

I came back to tell people what he would spend his life proving: that the background lead in modern people was many times higher than nature ever intended, that there is no safe level for a child's brain, and that the fuel industry would insist for twenty years that it was harmless.

I had one advantage he didn't. I knew the alternatives worked. Higher-octane fuels without lead, better engine design, and catalytic converters that lead would have poisoned anyway.

I wrote to every newspaper and every member of Congress. The letters came back politely. A few mothers' groups listened, and asked their towns to test the soil around schools.

They found what he found. It just took the world another forty years to listen.

The letter

  1. Lead from petrol is in the air, the soil and our children's blood.
  2. There is no safe level for a child's brain.
  3. Engines can run without it: refine better fuel, redesign the engines.
  4. Test the soil around schools and homes. Then ban it.

A note on why

Leaded petrol was used for almost a century and exposed billions of people. The effects on children's development are severe, permanent and spread across a whole population.

The science existed by the 1950s and alternatives existed by the 1970s. What was missing was the will.

  • Tetraethyl lead was added to petrol from 1923.
  • Clair Patterson published the age of the Earth (about 4.55 billion years) in 1956 and, through that work, documented widespread lead contamination and campaigned against it.
  • The US began phasing out leaded petrol in the 1970s and banned it for cars in 1996. The last country stopped selling it in 2021.
  • A 2022 study estimated that lead from petrol cost Americans born since the 1940s hundreds of millions of IQ points in total.

Runners-up for this era: 'smoking causes cancer' (still contested then); polio vaccine (arriving anyway); seat belts.

The narrator and the mothers' groups are invented. Patterson's work, the history of leaded petrol and its phase-out are real. This is a story about history, not medical, legal or safety advice.
