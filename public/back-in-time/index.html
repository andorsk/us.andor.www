One Thing Back

If you could carry one thing back in time (a technology, a piece of knowledge, an idea, a habit), what would it be?

Two answers for every era. For the world: one technology or piece of knowledge that era could use straight away, told as a short story set in its time and styled like it. For me: the idea, habit or bit of hindsight that would give me a good life there. One era per century until 1800, then every twenty years, then every decade. Each page has the real history underneath.
